About CardiVyn AI

CardiVyn AI is an AI-powered heart disease prediction system developed using an Artificial Neural Network (ANN). It analyses patient health parameters to estimate the likelihood of heart disease.

How Does It Work?

Users enter 13 health-related parameters, including age, cholesterol, blood pressure and maximum heart rate. The trained ANN model processes these inputs and generates a prediction.

Technology Used

Python, TensorFlow, Keras, Scikit-learn, Flask, HTML and CSS.

Our Objective

To demonstrate how Artificial Intelligence can be used for preliminary heart disease risk assessment.

This application is for educational purposes only and does not replace professional medical advice.
